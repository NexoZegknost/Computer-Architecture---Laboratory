\subsection{Exercise 3}
\task{Requirement}
Print out the result of
\[
  F = \frac{(a+10)\times(b-d)\times(c-2\times a)}{a+b+c}
\]
where $a$, $b$, $c$, $d$ are integers inserted in this exact order
(prompts: ``Insert a: '', ``Insert b: '', \dots), $a+b+c \neq 0$.
Both the quotient and the remainder must be printed.

\task{Idea}
Describe your approach here (\texttt{mul}, \texttt{div}, \texttt{mflo}, \texttt{mfhi}).

\task{Verification}
With $a=3$, $b=4$, $c=5$, $d=6$:
\[
  F = \frac{13 \times (-2) \times (-1)}{12} = \frac{26}{12}
  \;\Rightarrow\; \text{quotient } 2,\ \text{remainder } 2.
\]
