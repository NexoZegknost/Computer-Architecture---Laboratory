\subsection{Exercise 4}
\task{Requirement}
Print the 4-bit binary value of a positive integer less than 16 entered by the
user (prompt: ``Please enter a positive integer less than 16: '').
For example, input \textbf{10} gives \textbf{Its binary form is: 1010}.

\task{Idea}
Since only arithmetic instructions are allowed, each bit $b_i$ ($i = 3,2,1,0$)
is obtained by integer division:
\[
  b_i = \left\lfloor \frac{n}{2^i} \right\rfloor \bmod 2
\]
Dividing by $8$, $4$, $2$ with \texttt{div} gives the quotient in \texttt{LO}
and the remainder in \texttt{HI} (\texttt{mflo}, \texttt{mfhi}).
Describe your approach here.
