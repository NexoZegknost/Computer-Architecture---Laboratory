\subsection{Exercise 6}
\task{Requirement}
Calculate the average CPI of a pipelined system, given that the miss rate of the
instruction memory is 5\%, the miss rate of the data memory is 10\%. The program
has 1000 instructions, 100 of which are load and store instructions. The miss
penalty is 100 cycles.

\task{Solution}
The ideal pipeline has $\text{CPI}_{ideal} = 1$. Every instruction is fetched from
the instruction memory, while only load/store instructions ($100/1000 = 10\%$)
access the data memory.
\begin{align*}
  \text{Instruction miss stall} &= 0.05 \times 100 = 5 \text{ cycles/instr} \\
  \text{Data miss stall}        &= \frac{100}{1000} \times 0.1 \times 100 = 1 \text{ cycle/instr} \\
  \text{CPI}                    &= 1 + 5 + 1 = \mathbf{7}
\end{align*}
